# Critical defects

The defects this audit confirmed, with their fix and the test that pins
each one; then the defects deferred with a reason; then the suspected
defects that could not be confirmed offline. Severity follows the audit's
scale: critical = can lose capital or place an unintended live order now;
high = incorrect decisions, accounting or state that leads to loss or an
unsafe restart; medium = wrong records, degraded protection or misleading
operator information. No critical defect was found: no path places a live
order without the risk gate, and live trading stays off by default behind
a phrase. Line numbers refer to main `b1db421` as the lenses read it,
except the lifecycle rows (F-46 to F-49, D-7), whose lens read `35bba10`.
